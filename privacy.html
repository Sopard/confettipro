Confetti Pro Support Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Confetti Pro works entirely on your device. There's no account, your videos never leave your iPhone, and we don't collect, sell or track any of your data.

Who we are

Confetti Pro ("the app") is provided by Ndibe Raymond ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. Questions? Email Support@toolsHQ.app.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account and no sign-in, and it doesn't send information about you to us or to anyone else.

Your videos and projects. Videos you choose are edited on your device and are never uploaded. Projects, and the name you can optionally add in Settings, are stored only on your device.

No tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and doesn't collect your location, contacts, browsing history or advertising identifier.

Photos

You pick videos with the system photo picker, which shares only the video you choose, so the app doesn't need access to the rest of your library. When you export, the app asks for permission to add the finished video to your library; it can't read your library with that permission. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Purchases

Confetti Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment and shares only a record that the purchase was made, which the app checks on your device. We never receive your payment details. Apple's own handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply, and delete them once they're no longer needed. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the GDPR in the EU/UK, or state laws in the US such as the CCPA), you may have rights over personal information about you. Because the app doesn't collect any, there's usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete; to remove everything the app has stored, delete the app. For any request, email Support@toolsHQ.app.

Children

Confetti Pro is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and it doesn't knowingly collect anyone's personal information.

Changes to this policy

If a future version handles information differently, for example by adding an optional account, we'll update this policy and tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
