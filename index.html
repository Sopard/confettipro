Confetti Pro Support

Need a hand? Email Support@toolsHQ.app and we'll get back to you. In the app, you can also go to Settings → Help → Report a problem, which fills in your app and iOS version for you.

How do I add confetti to a video?

On the Home screen, choose a video from your library. Pick an effect (Shower, Burst, Popper, Pop or Fireworks), then use the tabs to set its timing, style, colours, motion and look. You can add up to 16 confetti moments to one video.

How do I move a moment to a different time?

Press and hold its mark on the timeline, then drag it. The video follows along so you can drop it on the exact frame. Drag either end of a shower to trim it. Pinch the timeline to zoom in.

How do I aim a burst, pop or fireworks?

Drag the handle on the video to where the confetti should come from.

What's free, and what does Confetti Pro unlock?

Editing and previewing are free. Exporting finished videos requires Confetti Pro, a one-time purchase. It isn't a subscription: you pay once and nothing renews.

I bought Confetti Pro. How do I get it on another device?

Sign in to the same Apple Account, open the Confetti Pro screen (tap Export, or Settings → Confetti Pro) and tap Restore. Restoring is free.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My export won't save to Photos

Confetti Pro needs permission to add videos to your library. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Confetti Pro and allow adding photos. You can also use Share to send the video straight to another app.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
